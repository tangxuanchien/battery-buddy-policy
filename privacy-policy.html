Privacy Policy

Battery Buddy: Emoji Battery · Effective date: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Battery Buddy: Emoji Battery app (package com.txclabs.emoji.battery, "the App"), published by TXC Labs ("we", "us"), handles information when you use it. We built the App to work with as little data as possible: there are no accounts, no ads and no in-app purchases.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, or payment information. The App does not show advertising and does not use advertising identifiers.

2. Information processed on your device only

  • Your customizations – chosen battery icons, stickers, status bar, notch and mascot settings, bookmarks and preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • Images you add – photos or GIFs you pick to use as stickers, icons or backgrounds are copied into the App's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded to us.
  • Installed app list – if you use the "exclude apps" feature, the App reads the list of launchable apps on your device so you can choose where overlays should be hidden. This list stays on your device.

3. Accessibility Service

The App offers an optional Accessibility Service, which you must turn on yourself in Android Settings. It is used solely to draw the custom status bar, battery icon, notch and stickers on top of other apps, and to hide them in apps you have excluded. To do that it only receives the name of the app currently in the foreground. It cannot read screen content (window content retrieval is disabled), does not record what you type or view, and nothing it receives is stored or sent off your device. You can turn it off at any time in Settings > Accessibility.

4. Other permissions

  • Display over other apps – to show the animated mascot (shimeji) on your screen.
  • Notifications / foreground service – to keep the mascot overlay running and let you stop it from the notification.
  • Internet – to download catalogs of icons, stickers and mascots.

5. Information collected automatically

The App uses the following Google Firebase services to understand usage and fix problems:

  • Firebase Analytics – anonymous usage events such as which screens are opened, app version, device model, OS version, language/country and an app-instance identifier.
  • Firebase Crashlytics – crash reports containing technical information (stack trace, device model, OS version, app state at the time of the crash).

This data is not linked to your identity and is used only to improve the App. Google processes it under the Firebase privacy and security terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

When the App downloads content (icons, stickers, mascots) from our content delivery network (Bunny.net), the CDN receives your IP address and standard request information as part of normal internet traffic. It is used only to deliver the files.

6. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. Other than the service providers described above, we do not share information with third parties, except where required by law.

7. Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device remains until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Analytics and crash data are retained by Firebase according to its retention settings (no longer than 14 months for analytics). You can request deletion of any data associated with your app installation by contacting us at the address below.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Network requests are made over HTTPS where supported. While no method is 100% secure, we take reasonable measures to protect the limited data we process.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

11. Contact

If you have questions or requests regarding this policy, contact us at tangxuanchien511@gmail.com.
